    About

    A fan-made viewer for mixing and matching Monster Hunter Generations Ultimate armor. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Capcom.

    Game content

    Monster Hunter Generations Ultimate and all of its models, textures, animations, armor names and data tables are © CAPCOM CO., LTD. All rights reserved. Every asset here was extracted from a personally owned copy of the game and is shown for reference only.

    Tools used to build this

    • RevilLib / RevilToolset — by PredatorCZ (Lukas Cone) Unpacked the .arc archives and converted MT Framework .mod meshes to glTF, .tex textures to DDS, .lmt motion lists to animation, and .plpartsdisp to XML.
    • hactool Unpacked the game's romfs and executable from the cartridge image.
    • three.js Renders the scene, with GLTFLoader, OrbitControls and SkeletonUtils.
    • Pillow Converted the extracted textures to PNG during the build.
    • MHGU-Modding wiki — by RTHKKona and contributors, ported from GReinoso96 and Aradi147's XXModding Documented the model and texture extraction route this project followed, and the weapon data table layout.

    Assets

    Monster icons, UI textures and the MHFU font are shared with the sibling MHGU fan apps listed under Other MHGU Apps. Armor names, rarities and color data are read from the game's own tables.
